# Change log: "Hora" tab — planetary hours

**Implements:** [plans/20260723_073637_hora-tab.md](../plans/20260723_073637_hora-tab.md)

## What was added

A fourth bottom tab named **Hora**. It shows the 24 planetary hours of today:
12 day horās (sunrise → sunset split into 12) and 12 night horās (sunset →
next sunrise split into 12). Each horā is ruled by one of the 7 classical
planets. The first day horā is ruled by the weekday's lord (Sunday → Sūrya,
Friday → Śukra, …); the rest step through the fixed horā order
`Sūrya → Śukra → Budha → Chandra → Śani → Guru → Maṅgala` and repeat.

The tab shows:

1. A **"Current horā"** card on top — the ruling planet now, day/night, its
   span, and how long it has left.
2. **Day horās** — 12 rows.
3. **Night horās** — 12 rows.

The row holding "now" is highlighted, tagged with a "Now" pill, and scrolled
into view on open. The highlight follows the clock at minute precision, so the
screen does not rebuild every second.

**Fallback (hard rule 4):** horās need a real sunset. With no location or on a
polar/fallback day the provider returns an empty list and the screen shows a
friendly "set a location" message — no fake times.

## Files added

- `lib/core/constants/hora_names.dart` — the 7 planet names, the fixed horā
  order, the weekday → starting-lord table, and `lordAt(weekday, k)`.
- `lib/models/hora_window.dart` — immutable `HoraWindow` (lord, isDay, UTC
  start/end, `contains`).
- `lib/services/hora_calculator.dart` — pure `HoraCalculator.horaList(...)`:
  splits each half of the day into 12 in microseconds with rounded boundaries
  (no gaps), returns empty on a non-positive half.
- `lib/providers/hora_providers.dart` — `horaDayProvider`: watches the clock's
  anchor sunrise/span via `select` (recomputes once per day roll or location
  change, not per tick), computes sunset once, returns the 24 horās.
- `lib/screens/hora_screen.dart` — the tab UI.
- `test/services/hora_calculator_test.dart` — 9 tests: 60-min horās on a
  12h/12h day, day-first ordering, gap-free tiling, the weekday lord for all 7
  weekdays, the fixed-order stepping, the 25th-horā self-consistency check,
  empty on non-positive day/night, uneven 14h/10h halves.
